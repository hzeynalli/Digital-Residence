Four demand pipelines

Karabakh & East Zangezur. $14.7bn invested 2021–25, heading for ~$18bn by 2028, with a further ~$7.9bn budgeted for 2026–29. Over 50,000 residents returned across 28 settlements; 18 hotels operating — the first international flag, Hilton Garden Inn Aghdam, opened December 2025.

A capital-wide master plan. A 93.6bn-AZN (~$55bn) programme approved December 2023 — renewal of ageing stock plus new mixed-use districts through 2040, roughly two-thirds financed from the state budget.

Caspian developments. Sea Breeze, Crescent Bay, White City, Brabus Island — millions of square metres of premium residential and hospitality, profiled below.

Tourism & hospitality. ~2.6m foreign visitors a year; travel & tourism at 8.2% of GDP (2025, WTTC). National room stock grew 10.7% in 2024 — about 3,100 keys added in a single year.

B2B department

The point

A B2B customer outfitting a hotel, restaurant or residence can already source furniture, kitchens, lighting, textiles, tableware and décor through Italdizain. Sanitaryware is the one missing category. Adding Villeroy & Boch Bathroom & Wellness completes the offer — and the value case holds for each of the three parties below.

For Italdizain

Completes the interior offer and deepens the share of every B2B project already in house.

For Villeroy & Boch

A thirty-year partner, immediate premium retail presence and a project channel — in a brand-safe environment.

For the customer

One accountable partner for the full interior — from specification to installation to after-sales.

Three sales drivers

  • 1The showroom. The AF Mall zone doubles as the B2B stage — architects, developers and operators see the range in room-set form, beside the design studio that specifies it.
  • 2Dedicated salespeople. Selling the brand into the project pipeline of Section 01 — starting from reference accounts PMD Hospitality and Absheron Hotels Group, and the PASHA / Saffron venue base.
  • 3After-sales as a channel. The in-house team trains service partners across the country; certified partners service the sanitaryware first — and then begin selling it themselves. National reach without payroll.

Phases run from launch: Phase II begins two to three years after Phase I starts and covers years three to five. Mass social housing and budget tenders are excluded — outside the brand's positioning.

  • aInstallation & commissioning by Italdizain's own service team — nothing outsourced.
  • bPrior experience with the range from an earlier period of cooperation circa fifteen years ago, refreshed through V&B technical training and certification.
  • cWarranty administration — one point of contact, managed on the brand's behalf.
  • dTrain the trainer. Once current, the team teaches partners across the country to service the sanitaryware — and, in time, to sell it. Service coverage becomes a distribution network.

In-house service is itself the differentiator: price-led importers do not service what they sell.
